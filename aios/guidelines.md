# Sprint 0024 — Tarot Depth & Polish

## Vision

The Tarot module is functional end-to-end, but "functional" is not the bar. The bar is **ritual**. A user opening a Celtic Cross spread should feel the same hush they'd feel sitting across from a real reader: the deck has weight, the shuffle has intention, every card lands with meaning, and the final synthesis reads like it was written *for them* — naming their actual situation, their actual chart, their actual moment — not a horoscope-grade aphorism dressed in tarot vocabulary. Visual polish, motion timing, copy, card-art fit, glow choreography, reveal pacing, deck physics, the silence between beats — all of it is in scope. So is the interpretation layer: generic, hedging, or pattern-matched language is a defect. If a reading could plausibly apply to any other person on the platform, it is not done. Hold a high taste bar — prefer fewer, sharper sentences over verbose ones; prefer concrete imagery over abstractions; prefer one true thing the user didn't already know over five plausible things they did. Keep the interface user-friendly throughout: discoverable, forgiving, never overwhelming, and never an info dump.

## Guidelines

Go deep on tarot itself — the cards' classical Rider–Waite–Smith symbolism, **how meanings shift by position** in a spread (a Tower in "Foundation" is not a Tower in "Outcome"), **card-to-card combinations** (elemental dignities, court-card dynamics, Major/Minor dialogue, why cards 1+2 form a unified central image), and how experienced readers weave ten cards into a single narrative rather than ten disconnected mini-readings. Then go deeper on the bridge to astrology: every card has planetary, elemental, and zodiacal correspondences, and the real insight lives in how those correspondences land against **this user's natal chart** (sign/house/degree of relevant placements, dominant element, chart ruler, current life chapter) and against **the user's transits today and through the week ahead** — the planets currently activating their natal placements, the lunar phase as it touches their chart, retrogrades hitting their personal points. These layers should connect seamlessly in the synthesis, not as separate "astrology section" and "tarot section" but as one voice. Critically, the reading must also be anchored to **what the user came to explore** — their original question, the area of life they selected, the emotional charge behind the draw — and that intent should be threaded explicitly through the interpretation, the GPT prompt payload, the chapter framing, and the Discuss ✦ follow-ups. Surface all this depth in a user-friendly way: do not dump the research into the UI, do not list every correspondence, do not explain the tradition unless asked. Distill ruthlessly — show only the few connections that actually illuminate this person's question at this moment, said in a way that lands.

## How to

Evaluate the current tarot implementation in its whole, then understand what are the next steps that would make it more beautiful, powerful and fluid. Insight generation should go deeper and deeper without becoming just empty words spit out, the depth of insight should find better and stronger correlations between everything, and only decide to print the most insightful one with th most supporting combination of elements, or the strongest and clearest ones.
